**Course**: 61FIT3MUL / 61MME3MUL Multimedia · Hanoi University  
**Theme**: 1 — Scam-Proof Family  
**Project Title**: *Safe Call, Safe Family: Outsmarting the Deepfake Emergency Scam*  
**Target Audience**: Parents and grandparents  
**Team Composition**: 3 Members (Member A, Member B, Member C)  
**Production Constraints**:
- **Zero Budget**: No rented equipment, no purchased props, no paid sets.
- **Locations**: 100% free, accessible university & dorm spaces (dorm desk, campus hallway/bench, empty classroom whiteboard).
- **Props**: Only personal everyday items students already carry (smartphones, student backpack).
- **Lighting & Audio**: Natural window daylight / classroom ambient lighting + smartphone built-in microphones.
- **Cast Rule**: All 3 members appear on camera; strictly **maximum 2 persons per scene**.

## 1. REALISTIC STUDENT-CENTRIC CONCEPT & ANGLE

### 1.1 The Angle (Student Feasibility)
A student’s mother/parent (played by Member A in casual clothes at a plain study desk) receives an urgent call from someone claiming to be her son in college (Member B), saying he had a motor accident near school and needs an emergency hospital transfer. The scammer (Member C) uses an AI voice clone clip. The parent stays calm, hangs up, and contacts her son directly. He is safely sitting in an empty university classroom. Together, they explain to parents and grandparents watching how to avoid falling for this trap.
